# Encounters

## Sub-features
Per-chapter invitation: sail with the dhow (camera follows the sailing dhow), open the carved door (leaves swing, warm light), find the colobus (camera finds it, it hops), walk the glowing tide (plankton brightens). Second tap returns.

## How to get to it (user POV)
Tap the pill under each scene, or tap the dhow/door/colobus itself in the world.

## Driving it with Playwright
Click `#encounter`, wait, screenshot; click again to return. Raycast picking: tap the object center on screen.

## Gotchas
The door sits at the alley's end by design; verify it is the carved teak door (frame, studs), not a wall. Encounter text replaces the scene title - check contrast against the live scene.
